# Retention Offers (Illustrative Policy)

## Eligibility for a retention offer

Retention offers are made only to customers flagged as high churn risk by the churn model and who have at least 3 months of tenure. Offers are not made to customers with an open fraud case.

## Offer types

A monthly-contract customer may be offered a switch to a one-year contract with a 5 percent discount for the first 6 months. A customer with two or more support calls in 90 days may be offered a free priority support upgrade for 3 months. A prepaid Basic customer may be offered a one-time upgrade to Prepaid Plus at the Basic price for 1 month.

## Contact rules

A customer who has received a retention offer in the last 30 days should not be contacted again with another offer. Outbound calls for retention follow the do-not-disturb rules and are restricted to 9 AM to 8 PM local time.

## Measurement

Retention campaigns are evaluated against a holdout group of at least 10 percent of eligible customers, so that the uplift in 90-day retention is measured rather than assumed.
