# Fraud and Security (Illustrative Policy)

## Transaction review

Transactions with a fraud probability at or above the configured threshold are placed in the review queue. Reviewers can release the transaction, block it or request customer verification. The review budget is set so that the queue stays within the team's daily capacity.

## Customer verification

When a transaction is held, the customer is verified with a one-time password sent to the registered mobile number. Three failed verification attempts lock the transaction for 24 hours.

## New device and SIM swap

A login from a new device after a SIM change is treated as high risk. The account is restricted to balance checks for 48 hours unless the customer completes in-person or video verification.

## Data handling

Customer and transaction data is used only for service, billing and fraud prevention. Models are retrained quarterly, and access to individual-level scores is limited to the fraud and retention teams.
