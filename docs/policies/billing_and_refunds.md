# Billing and Refunds (Illustrative Policy)

## Dispute window

A customer can raise a billing dispute within 60 days of the bill date. Disputes raised later are reviewed case by case by a supervisor.

## Refund eligibility

A duplicate charge is refunded in full within 7 working days to the original payment method. A charge for a service the customer did not activate is refunded in full after verification. Refunds for usage charges are assessed against call and data logs.

## Credit adjustments

Service outages longer than 4 consecutive hours qualify for a credit equal to one day of the monthly charge. Credits are applied to the next bill and cannot be paid out in cash.

## Payment methods

UPI, card and netbanking payments are credited within 24 hours. Cash payments made at partner outlets are credited within 48 hours.
