# Prepaid Plans (Illustrative Policy)

## Plan tiers

Prepaid Basic is priced at INR 199 per month with a fixed data allowance. Prepaid Plus is priced at INR 399 per month and includes higher data, a priority support queue and free caller tunes.

## Validity and renewal

A prepaid pack stays valid for 28 days from activation. A customer can renew up to 3 days before expiry without losing the unused balance. If a pack lapses, unused data expires and is not carried forward.

## Upgrading from prepaid to postpaid

A prepaid customer with at least 6 months of tenure and no late recharges in the last 6 months can request a postpaid upgrade from the app. The upgrade needs a valid card or a verified UPI mandate. The first postpaid bill is pro-rated from the upgrade date.
