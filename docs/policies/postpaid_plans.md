# Postpaid Plans (Illustrative Policy)

## Plan tiers

Postpaid 5G is priced at INR 699 per month and includes unlimited calling, a high-speed data cap and international roaming packs on request. Postpaid Family is priced at INR 1299 per month for up to four connections on one account, with a shared data pool.

## Contract terms

Monthly plans have no lock-in period. A one-year contract gives a 5 percent discount on the monthly charge. A two-year contract gives a 10 percent discount and a device subsidy when a new handset is bought on the plan.

## Early termination

Customers on one-year or two-year contracts who leave before the term ends pay the remaining device subsidy, prorated by months left. Monthly plan customers can leave at any time after giving 7 days notice.

## Late payments

A bill unpaid after its due date by 5 days receives a reminder SMS. If it remains unpaid after 15 days, the account is moved to restricted service. Two late payments in twelve months trigger a review of the customer's payment method.
